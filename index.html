About Me

Digital Operations & ITSM leader with 17+ years of experience.

I am a techno-functional IT professional with 17+ years of experience across IT service management, problem management, service governance, Salesforce administration, reporting and analytics, and Agile delivery. I have worked extensively in regulated environments, including banking operations and enterprise IT, as well as service-driven organisations. This background gives me a practical, risk-aware perspective on how governance must translate into operational clarity, service reliability, and stakeholder confidence.

My core strength lies in reducing operational risk by transforming fragmented, manual work into structured workflows, reliable reporting, and disciplined execution. I specialise in identifying where work gets stuck—recurring incidents, unclear ownership, poor visibility, or weak controls—and improving those systems through documentation, dashboards, automation, and cross-functional alignment. This enables predictable outcomes, faster decision-making, and audit-ready operations.

In recent roles, I work at the intersection of problem management, analytics, and delivery governance, using tools such as Civica Service Desk, Azure DevOps, Power BI, and Salesforce to strengthen operational control and transparency. I have led through high-pressure situations, resolved critical service issues, and improved leadership visibility through better reporting and execution discipline. I am particularly interested in senior delivery and advisory roles where organisations need stronger IT governance, automation-led efficiency, cybersecurity awareness, and dependable service outcomes—especially in regulated or scaling environments.
